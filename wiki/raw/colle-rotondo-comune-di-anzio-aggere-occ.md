Abstract
This paper describes some preliminary results of the research project on the Lain foriied
setlement of Colle Rotondo which has been carried out by the Universiies of Rome Sapien-
za, Tor Vergata and Roma Tre between 2009 and 2012.
The research surveyed two lines of foriicaion of the setlement: the irst one, daing to
the early Iron Age, is evidenced by an agger (rampart) located in the eastern side of the
plateau, the second one includes an archaic (6th century BC) agger located in the interior of
the plateau.
This inner foriicaion had two phases in the maintenance of the rampart: the irst one
occurred in the 6th century BC, the later one in the 5th century BC which consists of a most
comprehensive foriicaion of the rampart with granular tufa blocks lining the walls of the
moat.
