     SUMMARY. - I n this work the A. studies the two geminate towns
of Anzio and Nettuno: the rising and fa11 of the famous harbour of Anzio,
formerly of the Volscians and then of Imperial Rome; the rising of Nettuno

- as a fortified Castle along the coast of Latium - when Anzio had been
abandoned by its inhabitants on account of the pressing barbaric and piratic

     The A. then makes a survey of the events and function of Nettuno
for the defence on the littoral of the Papa1 Dominion, unti1 the resurrection
of Anzio, thanks to Pope Innocente XII who in 1700 built there a new
harbour, using the left pier of the ancient Neronian harbour.
     After considering the vicissitudes of the new harbour up to our days,
the A. makes a detailed study of al1 the aspects (demographic, commercial,
industria1 and above al1 agricultural and bathing-touristic as well as town-
strutture) of the life of the two town, which are today two favourite
bathing-resorts of the Romans.
     After extending this study also to their respective municipal districts,
the A. searches and points out the causes of the fast development of Anzio
and Nettuno, which began early in the XX century.
